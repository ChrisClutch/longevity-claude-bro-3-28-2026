Protocol #1

MOVE
SWEAT
SLEEP

Three lifestyle interventions that beat every supplement combined. No gear required. No subscription. Just science.

Lifestyle #1 · Non-negotiable
Zone 2 Cardio

Exercise at a pace where you can talk but don't want to. This specific zone triggers the biggest mitochondrial response. Think brisk hike, easy jog, bike. Not HIIT. Not strolling. That 70–75% heart rate zone is where your cells literally grow new power plants.

The protocol: 4 sessions per week. 45–60 minutes each. That's it. Do this for 6 months and retest your resting heart rate and energy levels — you won't recognize them.

5× lower all-cause mortality, top vs bottom fitness quintile
Lifestyle #2 · Underrated killer app
Finnish Sauna Protocol

A 20-year Finnish study tracked 2,000 men. Those who used the sauna 4–7 times per week had a 40% lower risk of dying from heart disease and — this is the part that should make you sit up — a 66% lower risk of Alzheimer's. Sixty-six percent. No drug does that.

The protocol: 80–100°C (176–212°F) for 15–20 minutes. Real dry sauna, not the lukewarm steam room at your gym. 4× per week minimum.

66% reduced Alzheimer's risk · 4–7×/week
Lifestyle #3 · The forgotten one
Deep Sleep Architecture

Your brain has a sewage system. It only runs during deep sleep. While you're out, cerebrospinal fluid floods your brain and flushes out amyloid-beta — the protein that builds up into Alzheimer's plaques. One bad night measurably increases plaque accumulation. This isn't metaphor. It's plumbing.

The protocol: Room temp 65–68°F. Zero alcohol within 3 hours of bed (one drink suppresses deep sleep 20–30%). Same wake time daily. Add magnesium glycinate at night.

50% less deep sleep at 65 vs 25 — but this is reversible

Three dietary approaches with actual human data behind them. Not trends. Not elimination cults. Frameworks that move epigenetic clocks.

Rank #1 · Strongest human evidence
Mediterranean-Plus (real version)

Not pasta and wine. The actual signal: high-polyphenol extra-virgin olive oil (>300mg/kg — most US brands don't qualify), wild fish, nuts, tons of vegetables, zero ultra-processed food. The PREDIMED trial: 7,447 people, 30% fewer heart attacks. Telomere data: longer telomeres in highest-adherence group. Warning: 70% of "EVOO" in US stores is diluted with cheaper seed oils. Source matters.

→ Action: Replace all cooking oils with quality EVOO. Test it: real stuff is slightly cloudy and burns your throat.

Rank #2 · Epigenetic clock mover
Fasting-Mimicking Diet Cycles

5 days per month: ~800–1,100 calories, mostly plant-based, low protein, low carb. This tricks your cells into thinking they're fasting. mTOR shuts down. Autophagy kicks in. Your body starts cleaning house — killing damaged cells, recycling broken parts. After 3 monthly cycles in a clinical trial: median 2.5-year biological age reduction on epigenetic clocks. That's not a supplement. That's time travel.

→ Action: Try one FMD cycle. Apps like Zero + meal prep make this manageable.
🔄
Rank #3 · Circadian + epigenetic
Time-Restricted Eating + Ketosis

Eat within a 6–10 hour window, aligned with daylight. The mechanism isn't just calories — it resets your cellular clocks. Add light ketosis (cut refined carbs, increase olive oil and avocado) and you get beta-hydroxybutyrate, a signaling molecule that literally reprograms which genes get turned on. It directly activates FOXO3a — the "longevity gene" found in centenarians worldwide.

→ Action: Stop eating by 7–8pm. First meal at 10am–noon. Try it for 3 weeks.

10 compounds that survived adversarial review across 7 AI models. Ranked by actual human evidence, not supplement industry hype. Tap each to expand.

First, the honest truth

Resveratrol? Debunked. Most NMN products? Degraded in the bottle. The supplement market runs on your fear of death. This list is what survives when you strip the hype. GlyNAC is $30 at any health food store and has better human trial data than compounds marketed at $200/month.

01
GlyNAC (Glycine + N-Acetylcysteine)
Reverses more aging hallmarks simultaneously than anything else with human RCT data. Baylor University. Multiple trials.
What it does: Restores your body's master antioxidant (glutathione), which drops sharply with age. Without it, your mitochondria drown in their own exhaust. The Baylor RCT corrected mitochondrial dysfunction, oxidative stress, inflammation, insulin resistance, endothelial dysfunction, and cognitive decline — simultaneously — in 24 weeks.

Why mainstream doesn't know it: It's two cheap amino acids. There's no $80/month product to market. No celebrity endorsement. Just science.

Dose: 8–10g Glycine + 600–1200mg NAC daily
9/10 confidence

02
Urolithin A
Phase 2 RCT data. Cleans out your broken mitochondria through a process called mitophagy. Only 40% of people produce it naturally.
What it does: Activates mitophagy — the selective garbage collection of broken mitochondria. Your cells accumulate dysfunctional power plants over decades. Urolithin A triggers the cleanup crew. Phase 2 RCT (Timeline Biosciences, 2022): direct improvement in mitochondrial function in human muscle tissue.

The catch: You're supposed to produce this from pomegranates via gut bacteria. But ~60% of people don't have the right gut bacteria to do the conversion. Supplementing bypasses the broken factory.

Dose: 500–1000mg/day (Mitopure brand has the trial data)
8/10 confidence

03
Magnesium Glycinate or Threonate
68% of US men are deficient. Cofactor in DNA repair, ATP production, and telomere maintenance. Costs $12.
What it does: Magnesium is involved in over 300 enzymatic reactions — including the enzymes that repair your DNA after damage, produce ATP (your cellular fuel), and maintain telomere length. Being deficient is like running a factory with half the machines broken.

Form matters: Magnesium oxide (most cheap supplements) is barely absorbed. Glycinate form: best sleep and muscle benefits. Threonate: crosses the blood-brain barrier, specifically relevant for cognitive aging.

Dose: 300–400mg elemental magnesium nightly
9/10 confidence — foundational

04
Omega-3 EPA/DHA (Triglyceride Form)
Slows telomere shortening. Actively resolves inflammation rather than blocking it. But most fish oil in stores is already rancid.
What it does: EPA and DHA build the active "resolution" molecules (resolvins, protectins) that terminate inflammation. Not the same as blocking it — that's like stopping a flood vs. bailing the water out. JAMA 2010 study: highest DHA quartile had the slowest telomere shortening over 5 years. Direct biological aging measurement.

Critical warning: Oxidized fish oil is pro-inflammatory. The supplement market is full of rancid product. Check TOTOX value (under 26). Only buy triglyceride or re-esterified TG form. The cheap ethyl ester form is ~50% less bioavailable.

Dose: 2–4g EPA+DHA/day with a fatty meal
9/10 confidence

Taurine
June 2023 Science paper shocked the field. Declines 80% from youth to old age. Costs cents per day to replace.
What it does: A 2023 study in Science (the top scientific journal) showed taurine supplementation extended lifespan 10–12% in mice and macaques, and corrected multiple aging hallmarks. In humans, higher blood taurine correlated with lower biological age markers. Mechanisms: stabilizes mitochondrial membranes, suppresses cellular senescence, improves microbiome diversity.

Why you've heard of it from energy drinks: Red Bull uses it as a cheap filler. That's like knowing about penicillin only from your antibiotic gummy vitamins. The compound is serious.

Dose: 2–4g/day. Pure powder is cheapest.
7.5/10 confidence — strong animal data, growing human

06
Vitamin D3 + K2 MK-7 (always together)
D3 regulates 1,000+ genes including telomerase. K2 prevents the calcium from calcifying your arteries. Don't take one without the other.
Why together: Vitamin D3 ramps up calcium-regulating proteins that require K2 to function. D3 without K2 can theoretically drive calcium into arteries instead of bones. Not theoretical — it shows up in arterial stiffness studies.

What D3 does: The Vitamin D receptor is a transcription factor that governs ~1,000 genes, including hTERT (the enzyme that maintains your telomeres). Most people need 5,000–8,000 IU to reach therapeutic blood levels. The government "safe limit" of 4,000 IU is extremely conservative and not based on toxicity data. Test your blood — target 50–70 ng/mL.

Dose: 5,000–8,000 IU D3 + 200mcg K2 MK-7 daily. Test twice/year.
8.5/10 confidence

07
Creatine Monohydrate
Most studied supplement in existence. Everyone thinks it's for gym bros. It's actually one of the most validated neuroprotective compounds we have.
What it does for longevity: Creatine recycles ADP back to ATP — the energy currency every cell runs on. In aging brains and hearts, energy metabolism starts failing. Creatine stabilizes this. Hundreds of RCTs in older adults show it preserves muscle mass and functional performance. Brain data is emerging: neuroprotective, cognitive support under stress.

The overlooked angle: Sarcopenia (muscle loss with age) is directly linked to mitochondrial density decline. They're the same problem from different angles. Creatine is a cheap, safe amplifier of resistance training's muscle-preserving effects.

Dose: 3–5g/day. No loading phase needed. Cheapest form works.
9/10 confidence for muscle; 7/10 for neuro

08
Spermidine (with a big caveat)
20-year European study: highest dietary intake = 5 extra years of life. The caveat: most supplements contain near-zero actual spermidine. Test or don't bother.
What it does: Induces autophagy through a completely different pathway than fasting — meaning it works even in a fed state. The Kiechl cohort (European Heart Journal, 2018) found the highest quartile of dietary spermidine intake associated with 5+ additional years of life expectancy over 20 years. Japanese natto consumption (fermented soybean, richest dietary source) tracks with Japanese longevity data.

The fraud problem: Independent lab testing of "wheat germ extract" supplements finds most contain virtually undetectable spermidine levels. Demand a third-party COA with quantified spermidine content. Without that verification, you're buying expensive starch.

Dose: 1–10mg/day verified. Or just eat natto.
7/10 confidence — verify your source

09
Astaxanthin (natural only)
The most powerful antioxidant known. 6,000× stronger than Vitamin C. Spans the entire cell membrane — inside and outside. Synthetic version doesn't work.
What it does: Most antioxidants work in either the water-based or fat-based parts of your cells. Astaxanthin spans the entire lipid bilayer — protecting mitochondrial membranes from both inside and outside simultaneously. Reduces 8-OHdG (the most validated marker of DNA oxidative damage) in human studies.

The sourcing is everything: Synthetic astaxanthin has the wrong molecular structure (S,S configuration vs. natural 3S,3'S). Studies with synthetic versions consistently show weaker effects. Look for "Haematococcus pluvialis" on the label — that's the algae source. Take with fat or absorption tanks.

Dose: 8–12mg/day with a fatty meal. H. pluvialis source only.
7/10 confidence

10
Microdose Lithium (1–5mg, not psychiatric dose)
The most ignored compound in longevity. Japanese towns with lithium in their water have 17% longer life expectancy. It's stigmatized by association with psychiatric dosing that's 300× higher.
What it does: At low doses, lithium inhibits GSK-3β — an enzyme that becomes overactive with age and drives tau protein buildup (Alzheimer's pathology). It also activates autophagy and elevates BDNF (your brain's growth hormone). Multiple cross-national studies show water supply lithium levels correlate with lower dementia rates, lower all-cause mortality. This association is geological — not socioeconomic — meaning it's hard to explain away as confound.

Why no one talks about it: Pure stigma. Psychiatric lithium carbonate is dosed at 600–1,800mg for bipolar disorder. The supplemental dose here (1–5mg lithium orotate) is a completely different pharmacological profile. Get periodic basic metabolic panels if you supplement long-term.

Dose: 1–5mg elemental lithium (as lithium orotate) daily
7/10 neuro; 6/10 systemic longevity

What kills the protocol

THE SILENT
SABOTEURS

You can run the perfect stack and eat like a monk. These four things will still quietly accelerate your aging if you don't address them first.

☠️
Glyphosate

Found in 80%+ of US urine samples. Disrupts gut microbiome, chelates manganese (breaks mitochondrial antioxidants), damages DNA. Eating organic for the Dirty Dozen isn't optional.

🛢️
Seed Oils

Linoleic acid accumulates in cell membranes. Oxidizes. Generates aldehydes that directly damage your mitochondrial electron transport chain. Full turnover takes 2 years. Start now.

🧴
PFAS / Forever Chemicals

In virtually every American's blood. Impair thyroid function, liver detox, and immune response. Non-stick cookware, food packaging, water. Filter your water. Ditch Teflon.

🧬
Microplastics

NEJM 2024: found in arterial plaques. Higher burden = 4.5× more cardiovascular events. They're in your blood, your organs, your brain. Avoid plastic food contact. Filter water.

The hierarchy

DO THIS
IN ORDER

The biggest mistake in the longevity space: adding supplements before fixing the foundation. The order of operations matters more than the ingredient list.

Remove the daily mitochondrial insults

Organic produce for high-pesticide items. Water filter (solid-block carbon or RO). Eliminate seed oils — replace with olive oil and animal fats. Ditch non-stick cookware. This is the foundation. Nothing else works optimally on top of daily chemical damage.

Build the aerobic base with Zone 2

The 5× mortality differential from VO2max dwarfs every supplement combined. Four sessions per week, 45–60 minutes. This is non-negotiable. Build this first. Everything else amplifies it.

Fix the dietary framework

Mediterranean-plus quality, periodic fasting. These are the only interventions that have moved epigenetic clocks in human clinical trials. The diet is not support infrastructure for the supplements — it's the other way around.

Add the evidence-backed short stack

GlyNAC. Urolithin A. Magnesium glycinate. Quality omega-3. Creatine. Vitamin D3+K2. These have actual human data. Don't buy anything else until these are dialed in.

Add sauna as a physiological intervention

Treat it like a prescription. Four sessions per week at proper temperature. The 66% Alzheimer's risk reduction data is stronger than any pharmaceutical in prevention trials. It deserves that level of respect.

Layer in taurine, spermidine, astaxanthin, lithium

These are amplifiers on an already-functioning system. They're not foundations. If you're doing steps 1–5, adding these compounds to a working protocol produces compounding gains. Adding them to chaos produces expensive urine.
